Atmospheric digital painting. A tiny lone wooden cabin at the bottom centre with one warm amber lit window and a small glowing red carved stone charm hanging above its closed door. Thick pale grey mist fills everything. Towering over the cabin, half lost in the mist, the long jointed legs and the underside of a colossal six-legged creature whose body disappears upward out of frame, pale feelers hanging beneath it. Muted desaturated grey-blue palette with only two points of colour: the amber window and the red charm. Quiet dread, huge sense of scale, soft edges fading into fog. Square composition for a small app-icon-sized logo: one clear focal point, strong silhouette, readable at 64 pixels. No text, no letters, no watermark, no border. Original creature design, not based on any film or show.
