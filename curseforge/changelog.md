## 1.0.0

First release.

- The Hollow: unkillable night folk that break into any room not sealed behind a talisman, and escalate every three nights survived.
- The talisman: wards a sealed room, loses a night at every dawn, fed with mist essence.
- The mist: a drifting bank with Skitterers, Stingers, Striders and the Colossus inside it.
- Mist essence, with recipes for a talisman, a Mending book, an enchanted golden apple and a totem of undying.
- Character levels, three talent trees with an active ability each, and five skills that rise by use.
- Standard and hardcore death rules.
- Original and CC0 sound throughout.
