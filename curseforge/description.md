# From the Mist

A survival horror mod about lasting one more night.

Two things are trying to kill you, and the only defence against the first has to be paid for by walking into the second.

## The night

Every night the **Hollow** come. They look like people. They walk, they never hurry, and nothing you carry can kill them. They know where you are through any wall, and they will take a house apart block by block to reach you.

One thing stops them: a **talisman** hung on the wall inside a **sealed room**. Sealed means every door, trapdoor and gate shut, with no gap to the outside. Open the door after dark and the ward is gone until you close it. Behind a sealed ward they can only stand at the wall, watch, and knock. Behind a sealed ward is also the only place you can sleep.

Every dawn costs the talisman one night of its life. You start with a talisman that holds three.

The longer you survive, the worse they get. Every three nights the Hollow become faster, stronger and more numerous, break through walls sooner, and break harder materials.

## The mist

Every day or two a **bank of mist** forms a few hundred blocks away and drifts across the world. A horn sounds and you are told which direction it is coming from. You can get out of its way, wait indoors while it passes, or walk in.

Inside it:

- **Skitterers**: fast, low, six-legged pack hunters.
- **Stingers**: winged things with a venomous tail. They come to light, and they will go through a lit window.
- **Striders**: spider-legged leviathans the height of a tree line, crossing the world in a straight line with no interest in you. Whatever is under a foot when it lands dies.
- **The Colossus**: twice the size, on six pillar legs, with a foghorn call you hear long before you see anything. Stingers travel with it.

Nothing from the mist can leave it. Step out of the bank and they dissolve behind you.

## Why you go in

Skitterers and Stingers drop **mist essence**, and nothing else does. Essence recharges a talisman and crafts new ones. It also crafts a Mending book, an enchanted golden apple and a totem of undying, and mist creatures drop iron, gold, emeralds, diamonds and good experience besides.

So the loop is: build and prepare by day, seal the room by night, and when the mist comes, decide how much you need what is in it.

## Your character

Press **K** for the character screen.

- **Levels** from surviving nights and killing things. Each level is a talent point.
- **Three talent trees**: Warden (the night and the talisman), Hunter (the mist) and Survivor (staying alive). Each ends in an active ability: throw the Hollow back from you, thin the mist and mark what is in it, or a burst of speed and toughness.
- **Skills that rise by use**: Combat, Archery, Mining, Athletics and Endurance.

## Dying

- **Standard worlds**: you respawn with your hotbar, armour and off hand. Your pack stays where you fell, glowing, and never despawns. You keep your level, talents and skills.
- **Hardcore worlds**: one life. When it ends, the mod tells everyone how many nights you lasted.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Java **26.3**
- **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API**
- Needed on both the client and the server

## Good to know

- Starts a new world best. It works on an existing one, but the first night will not wait for you to be ready.
- Neither the Hollow nor the mist's creatures spawn on you deep underground. The Hollow dig.
- The `mobGriefing` game rule stops the Hollow breaking blocks, which removes most of the threat.
- Operators have `/mist start`, `/mist approach`, `/mist stop` and `/mist status` for trying things out.
- This is an early release. Balance will change, and the art is placeholder pixel work that will be redrawn.
